The Problem

A market built on opacity.

01 — Illiquid

Capital locked in stone

High-value emerald inventory sits idle with no access to capital markets. No mechanism exists to fractionalize ownership or enable broader investment participation.

02 — Opaque

No verifiable provenance

Certificates of authenticity are paper documents — easy to forge, impossible to verify globally, and entirely disconnected from any digital infrastructure.

03 — Fragmented

Value lost across the lifecycle

From raw lot to cut stone to finished jewelry, value is created at every step — yet no single party captures or represents that full arc on-chain.

This isn't just an emerald problem. It is the structural failure of every physical commodity market — coffee, wine, art, precious metals. Facet is the infrastructure to fix it.

What We Built

The full lifecycle,
on-chain.

Every step from raw physical lot to final NFT certificate is verifiable, immutable, and composable. Nothing is mocked. Every event is a real on-chain transaction.

01
Physical lot enters vault

The emerald lot arrives at our Medellín vault. Physical custody is established and documented before any on-chain action.

Medellín, Colombia
02
Registered on-chain

Location, custodian, weight, and certificate hash are written to RwaLotRegistry — permanent and immutable.

RwaLotRegistry
03
Fractionalized → ERC20

The lot is tokenized into FACET-LOT tokens at 1 token = 0.01 ct. Transferable and tradeable on the secondary market.

FACET-LOT · ERC20
04
Physical extraction → tokens BURN

When a stone is physically extracted, corresponding tokens are burned — a provable link between digital and physical.

Burn event · on-chain
05
NFT certificate minted

A unique FACET-EXTRACT ERC721 is minted with photo, metadata, and certificate of authenticity — the permanent on-chain replacement for paper certs.

FACET-EXTRACT · ERC721
